WearCost

The $200 jacket you've worn twice costs $100 per wear. The $60 jeans you've worn a hundred times cost $0.60. The price tag tells you what you paid - not what it costs.

WearCost is cost-per-wear truth for your closet. Add items with their prices, tap "wore it today" honestly, and the list ranks itself: unworn money traps on top, workhorses at the bottom. Shopping changes once you see the math.

No account, no cloud - everything stays in your browser.
